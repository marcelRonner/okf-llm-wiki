## What it says

The checklist evaluates the three required conditions in OKF v0.2 §11 and the recommended or optional provenance, trust, lifecycle, link, reserved-file, computation, versioning, and consumer-behaviour conventions. It reports that the assessed `content/` bundle conforms to the three required conditions as of 2026-09-11.

The assessment records optional features that are not adopted: per-claim source identifiers and credibility signals, verification events, trust-tier presentation, `stale_after`, bundle-relative links, a reserved `index.md`, and attested computations. It also limits local consumer evidence to the reader acceptance checks.
