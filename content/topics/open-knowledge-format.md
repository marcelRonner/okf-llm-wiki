# Open Knowledge Format

A specification from Google Cloud, v0.1 published 2026-06-12
([summary](../sources/2026-06-12-open-knowledge-format.md)), for packaging knowledge as a directory
of Markdown files with YAML frontmatter. It is the formalisation of the [LLM Wiki](llm-wiki.md)
pattern: where the gist describes an architecture, this defines a contract a bundle can be checked
against.

It is deliberately a format rather than a platform — no SDK, runtime, registry or manifest. `type`
is the only required frontmatter key. `title`, `description`, `resource` and `tags` are recommended,
and extra keys never break a consumer. `index.md` and `log.md` are reserved, both optional.

## What conformance means

Three criteria. Every non-reserved file has parseable frontmatter; every frontmatter carries a
non-empty `type`; reserved filenames follow their structure where present. Consumers must not reject
a bundle over missing optional fields, unknown types or broken links, which makes the bar genuinely
low and interoperability the point.

## Where this wiki stands, as of 2026-09-11

Measured over `content/`: **criterion one passes on every page**, and `log.md` satisfies criterion
three — it is already the chronological history the spec reserves that name for. `type` was on every
real page before the spec was read.

**Criterion two fails on five files.** The generated section pages — `topics/_index.md` and its
siblings — carry `title`, `description` and `weight` but no `type`, which a strict consumer reads as
five untyped concepts. They are generated by `make schema`, so the fix is one line.

That gap was closed on 2026-09-11, along with everything else the spec asks for that this wiki can
honestly give. Frontmatter now uses `description`, `sources` as a list of objects each with a
`resource`, `status` from the spec's own set, and `generated` stamped by `make index`. Source pages
carry a `resource` pointing at the original. `verified` is available and deliberately left for the
owner to fill, since an assistant asserting that the owner checked something would defeat the point
of recording it.

Two divergences remain, both deliberate: relative links rather than bundle-relative absolute ones,
which is what keeps the files working in Obsidian, and `_index.md` rather than `index.md`, which
Hugo requires.
